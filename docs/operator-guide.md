# Operator Guide — North Macedonia

Human-gated filing only.

Portal: ЕСЈН (Електронски систем за јавни набавки, e-nabavki.gov.mk),
administered by Биро за јавни набавки (bjn.gov.mk).

Before drafting or submitting a filing, confirm the engagement's own
ground truth on file:

- ЕМБС (Central Registry company registration number) verified
- ЕДБ (Public Revenue Office tax identity number) obtained
- unpaid taxes/contributions/public charges, if any, are covered by a
  deferred-payment arrangement that is BOTH approved by the tax
  authority AND currently being paid regularly (ЗЈН Член 88(2)(a)) —
  an approval alone, without ongoing payment, is not sufficient
- claimed engagement fee equals base fee + monitoring months × monthly
  rate

`:filing/draft` and `:filing/submit` always require your explicit
approval, even when the governor reports no violations.
